Forensically review the **entire Tazdan repository** and **tazdan.com**, then produce the definitive, evidence based institutional reference book on what Tazdan is, what is built, how it works, what is missing, and its commercial potential. Complete the files; do not stop at analysis or deliver a pitch deck.

Recursively inspect all applications and layers: mobile, web, backend, APIs and routes, controllers, services, repositories, middleware, authentication and permissions, admin and business portals, database schemas and migrations, financial ledger, wallets, payments, FX and rates, remittance, P2P, crypto and custody, KYC/AML and risk, cards, deposits and withdrawals, recurring payments, messaging and notifications, reconciliation and audit, monitoring, infrastructure and deployment, configuration and environment examples, Docker, dependencies, documentation, tests and stress tests, scripts, queues, Redis, Socket.IO, webhooks, workers, scheduled jobs, security and error handling, TODOs, deprecated code, and mock or partial integrations. Inspect every relevant public page on **https://tazdan.com** for positioning, branding, customer promises, product and business claims, target users, terminology, screenshots, conversion language, trust signals, and international positioning. Compare the site with implementation in **“Public Product Claims vs Technical Reality.”** Classify each claim as fully supported, substantially supported, partially supported, planned, partner dependent, regulatory dependent, or not evidenced.

For each major capability, distinguish **production implemented; implemented but needs hardening; partially implemented; frontend only or simulated; mock provider integration; planned or inferred; missing.** Cite repository paths, modules, schemas, routes, or services for technical claims. Trace all factual claims to code, the website, a verified public source, or a clearly labelled assumption. Label proposed designs and financial assumptions separately from verified facts. Where evidence is absent, write: **“Not verified in the reviewed implementation.”** Never invent functionality, legal status, partnerships, or citations.
