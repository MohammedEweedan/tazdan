TAZDAN / INVESTOR MEMORANDUM 04 / OPERATING MODEL
How the business will operate
Acquire and verify
1
A customer registers, completes required checks and agrees the approved terms.
Fund and quote
2
A supported bank or approved rail funds the wallet; Tazdan presents the executable price and fee.
Reserve and route
3
The ledger reserves funds; risk and limits determine whether to proceed or review.
Deliver and record
4
Internal credit or activated partner payout completes; earned fees and spread are recorded.
Reconcile and retain
5
Provider statements and ledgers reconcile; support, history and repeat use close the loop.
The proposed launch structure
Tazdan operates the product, technology, customer journey and control workflows. Authorised entities and
contracted banks, payout providers and liquidity venues perform the regulated activities within their agreed
legal scope. Entity structure, allocation of compliance duties and customer-fund ownership must be fixed in
contracts before activation.
Money and revenue are accounted for separately
Customer balances and transfer principal are obligations or settlement flows. Revenue consists of earned fees
and spread, not the value of money passing through the system. Corporate capital funds operating expenses
and prefunding; customer funds are not a source of business profit or funding.
This is the proposed commercial operating model, not a statement that approvals or contracts already exist. Manual bank confirmation
and payout operations require named staff, cutoffs and daily close procedures. [I3-I6]

Pricing is a commercial assumption
Remittance corridors have configurable spread, fixed and percentage fees; the model uses a 1.8% blended
realised fee/spread yield and does not add seeded flat fees. The LYD market maker uses pips, not a fixed
2.5% conversion charge: a 0.05 LYD half-spread would be 0.5% at a purely illustrative 10 LYD/USD reference.
Actual capture changes with rates, skew and inventory.
The crypto 2.5%, withdrawal $3 and business $0.50 are modeled realised pricing. They require approved
tariffs and correctly charged, reconciled fee postings. Business fees are configurable and can currently default
to zero. LYD swap margin journal wiring and testing are a launch prerequisite for that revenue line.
Excluded from base revenue: P2P escrow fees (currently zero), cards/interchange, subscriptions,
customer-balance yield, group-wallet or pool returns, DEX execution fees and white-label licensing.
These are optional future upside, not assumed cash flow.
