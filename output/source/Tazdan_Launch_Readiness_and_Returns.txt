The bank partnership must be concrete

A Libyan bank relationship could shorten the route to service launch and improve trust, distribution and settlement access. Ownership of a bank alone does not establish that every Tazdan product can operate under its authority. The bank’s compliance/legal team and regulator must confirm the precise structure.
Workstream | Proposed responsibility to agree
Regulated service and customer funds | Bank: permitted accounts/payment activities, funds custody/safeguarding, customer terms and required approvals. Confirm these duties in writing.
Technology and operations | Tazdan: app, APIs, ledger orchestration, support tooling, security, incident handling and contracted service delivery.
KYC/AML and reporting | Allocate onboarding, beneficial ownership, screening, transaction monitoring, investigations and regulatory reporting; identify the accountable officers.
Settlement and treasury | Confirm funding currencies, liquidity owner, netting, cutoffs, failure/reversal handling, losses and independent daily reconciliation.

Commercial terms that determine the forecast
Get a signed term sheet covering the bank fee/revenue share, direct rail prices, minimum guarantees, reserves, settlement credit, fraud liability and support responsibilities. The model assumes the bank takes <b>20% of the customer fee pool</b> in Base; execution/rail costs are separate. Replace both with the actual all-in contract and avoid double counting.
<b>Bank-supported case is conditional:</b> same Base customers, prices, bank share and company prefunding. Acquisition cost falls from $25 to $12 per activation, and fixed operating costs fall to $20k / $25k / $35k / $45k / $55k per month in Years 1–5 through shared services and distribution. None of that support is currently confirmed.
A minimum platform fee paid by the bank could improve downside protection. It is excluded until agreed. Cards, card funding, crypto custody and extra corridors remain outside the pilot unless their own providers and permissions are accepted.

How likely is success?

<b>Defined success:</b> at least three consecutive profitable operating months by M36, while meeting customer-money controls and avoiding additional equity funding. This is a narrower and more useful goal than “becoming a major fintech.”
Conditional milestone | Planning judgment
Obtain a workable bank/legal/service arrangement | 60–70%
Deliver a safe, reliable pilot given that arrangement | 80–90%
Achieve economics and adoption given the first two | 55–70%
Current combined judgment | About 25–45%
With signed viable bank support and confirmed cost sharing | About 45–65%
The first three ranges are subjective assumptions. Their conditional chain gives roughly 26–44% (0.60×0.80×0.55 to 0.70×0.90×0.70); this is not an empirical failure-rate model. With the first milestone achieved, the remaining chain is about 44–63%. Ranges are rounded for planning. There is no verified paying-customer cohort, conversion history or comparable-outcome dataset to calibrate them.
Risks that can change the answer
Risk | Practical mitigation
Bank terms / legal scope | Bind permitted products, costs, responsibilities and exit rights in contracts. A possible partnership is not an activated service.
Margin compression / competition | Pilot total-price acceptance. Existing banks, instant payment systems and licensed exchange channels already serve this market.
Security / money integrity | Resolve the reproduced test failure; complete DB rail tests, independent assurance and daily reconciliation.
FX / settlement liquidity | Executable quotes, exposure limits, prefunding thresholds and contracted liquidity; no profit assumption from the official/parallel gap.
Distribution / retention | Use measured bank-channel cohorts, repeat paid use and contribution. Pay only for acquired customers whose economics justify it.
Partner concentration / capital | Termination and data portability rights, backup rail plan, reserve policy and an agreed cost-cut trigger.
Treat the bank partnership as a measurable commercial advantage only when its terms deliver lower costs, reliable funding or contractual revenue. The software foundation alone is insufficient to justify a high success probability.

What the financial model assumes
All demand, pricing, cost and bank-share inputs are explicit assumptions. Base: launch M6; 20% bank share; 1.5% + $3 paid transaction pricing; 0.65% + $1.25 direct rail cost; 0.10% loss allowance; 4% monthly active churn; $25 CAC; $2 KYC at 75% check-to-activation. All Base years keep these rates; ticket and frequency increase as detailed on page 5. Bank-supported changes only CAC and fixed costs.
Corporate prefunding = paid volume × 40% externally settled × 2 days / 30 × 1.3 buffer, with at least $300k held. Tax is a conservative 20% reserve on positive monthly operating profit, with no loss credits; it is not a legal tax opinion. Fixed opex includes recurring people, compliance, support, infrastructure and vendor minimums. Customer principal, card income, deposit interest, token appreciation and exit value are excluded.
